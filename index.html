About Me

I am a Master in Information Technology graduate (Distinction, CGPA 3.79 / 4.0) with a First Class Honours degree in Mechatronics Engineering. My Master’s project focused on robot path planning, where I developed N-DQN-RRT, a hybrid algorithm that uses a Deep Q-Network to guide the sampling of an RRT* planner toward goal-directed, obstacle-aware routes.

The project included a 7-dimensional state representation and an adaptive rewiring radius that scales with learned obstacle clearance. I trained the agent for 400 episodes on procedurally generated 2D maze environments and validated it over 100 stochastic trials against baseline sampling strategies. The work was published in a peer-reviewed journal, the International Journal of Advanced Computer Science and Applications (IJACSA, 2026).

I also bring hands-on engineering experience from LOTTE Hotels Yangon, where I maintained HVAC systems, chillers and cooling towers, and from Mitsubishi Motors, where I worked on vehicle diagnostics and servicing. I am now looking for a maintenance engineering position or an internship in machine learning and artificial intelligence, and I am open to relocating for the right opportunity.
